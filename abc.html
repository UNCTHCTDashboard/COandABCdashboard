Area-Based Coordination (ABC)

Area-Based Coordination (ABC) is a practical coordination approach designed to operationalize collective outcomes at state and local level through stronger geographic prioritization, Government engagement, evidence-based planning, and coordinated action across humanitarian, development, and peace actors.

ABC Contribution to Collective Outcomes

How Area-Based Coordination supports Collective Outcomes

🌾

Food Security

Supports coordinated visibility and planning around localized food security, resilience, and livelihood priorities identified within state-level plans.

🏥

Basic Services

Supports integrated planning around health, education, WASH and public services within documented state priorities.

🏘

Durable Solutions

Supports coordination around state-level Durable Solutions roadmaps, return and reintegration priorities, settlement planning, and HLP-related interventions.

⚡

Rapid Response

Supports linkages between subnational coordination structures, partners, and localized operational planning where multi-sector engagement is required.

🕊

Peace & Governance

Supports visibility and coordination around Government-led structures, local stabilization, social cohesion, and protection-related priorities.
