Introduction

Area-Based Coordination and the Upper Nile Durable Solutions Roadmap

Area-Based Coordination (ABC) was established in South Sudan in 2023 under the auspices of the UN Resident and Humanitarian Coordinator, in three pilot states: Upper Nile, Unity and Western Bahr el Ghazal. ABC supports the transition from life-saving assistance toward longer-term recovery and durable solutions by strengthening coordination and complementarity across humanitarian, development, and peace actors through joint planning and localized engagement with Government and partners.

In 2025, building on progress from the pilot states and amid unprecedented funding cuts, the UN system and humanitarian leadership expanded ABC to all ten states in South Sudan in order to strengthen localized coordination, prioritization, and collective delivery despite growing resource constraints.

In Upper Nile State, ABC has supported operationalization of the Upper Nile Durable Solutions Roadmap (2024–2026) and its extension into the Tier II framework (2026–2028), in line with the national durable solutions strategy and plan of action for refugees, internally displaced persons, returnees and host communities.

Coordinated interventions through ABC have supported return, reintegration, and durable solutions efforts across areas including Malakal, Hai Matar, Hai Luakat, Fashoda, Nasir, Ulang, Baliet, Longochuk, and Maiwut.

Upper Nile roadmap priorities
  • Access to land and secure tenure for IDPs and returnees.
  • Establish safe and sustainable settlements for IDPs and returnees.
  • Promote social cohesion and peacebuilding to prevent new displacement.
  • Strengthen livelihoods and local economies.
  • Expand basic services.

Enabling activities supporting roadmap implementation

This section highlights key enabling activities contributing to implementation of the UNSRM framework and broader durable solutions progress across Upper Nile State.
