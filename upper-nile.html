Introduction

Area-Based Coordination and the Upper Nile Durable Solutions Roadmap

Area-Based Coordination (ABC) was established in South Sudan in 2023 under the auspices of the UN Resident and Humanitarian Coordinator, in three pilot states: Upper Nile, Unity and Western Bahr el Ghazal. ABC aims to facilitate the transition from life-saving assistance to longer-term solutions, enabling greater complementarity of humanitarian, development and peace actions through joint planning and programming with governments and partners at the local level.

In 2025, amidst unprecedented funding cuts and building on progress from the pilot states, the UN and humanitarian leadership pooled resources to scale up ABC to cover all ten states in South Sudan.

In Upper Nile State, ABC has enabled the operationalization of the Upper Nile Durable Solutions Roadmap (2024–2026) and its extension to Tier I (2026–2028), in line with the national durable solutions strategy and plan of action for refugees, internally displaced persons, returnees and host communities.

Coordinated interventions through ABC have supported returns and solutions work in areas including Malakal, Hai Matar, Hai Luakat, Fashoda, Nasir, Ulang, Baliet, Longochuk and Maiwut.

Upper Nile roadmap priorities
  • Access to land and secure tenure for IDPs and returnees.
  • Establish safe and sustainable settlements for IDPs and returnees.
  • Promote social cohesion and peacebuilding to prevent new displacement.
  • Strengthen livelihoods and local economies.
  • Expand basic services.
